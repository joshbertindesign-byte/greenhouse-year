# Greenhouse Year: play-time study

Five simulated players, 6 runs each, 20 in-game years per run. Times are median **real play time at 1x speed** (running periods plus time spent planning, at the homestead and on the year-end screens), with the median in-game year in brackets. "4/6 runs" means only that many runs got there within 20 years.

| Profile | Notices a new job after | Looks at the board every | Zones out | Plans a period in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lightning | 0.8 s | 0.5 s | never | ~15 s + 3 s per order |
| Sharp | 1.2 s | 1 s | rarely, 5 s | ~22 s + 4 s per order |
| Steady | 2 s | 2 s | sometimes, 8 s | ~30 s + 5 s per order |
| Casual | 3 s | 3.5 s | often, 10 s | ~40 s + 6 s per order; skips 8% of offers |
| Distracted | 4.5 s | 5 s | a lot, 15 s | ~50 s + 8 s per order; works jobs oldest-first, skips 15% of offers |
