# Greenhouse Year: long-term study (50 runs, until failure or year 40)

Five simulated players, 10 runs each, played until the nursery closed or 40 in-game years. Build: slower clock (pace 1.48), grower 5% slower, startup tax break, purchasable loading bays, crew wages −5% and the extra crew slots, rush-hire wage fix. Times are median **real play time at 1x speed** (running periods plus modeled planning, homestead and year-end time). `· 7/10` means only that many runs got there; the median is over those runs.
