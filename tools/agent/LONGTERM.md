# Greenhouse Year: long-term study (50 runs, until failure or year 40)

Five simulated players, 10 runs each, played until the nursery closed or 40 in-game years. Build: harder economy (crew wages +3% a year, each extra house costs more upkeep than the last, graduated income tax on profit after the 3-year tax break, crowded-market price drop past 45 flats of one crop in one period), harder late goals (bigger targets from level 10, several needing 2–3 years in a row), rivals that keep improving after year 14, grower XP −15%. Earlier: slower clock, purchasable loading bays, startup tax break. Times are median **real play time at 1x speed** (running periods plus modeled planning, homestead and year-end time). `· 7/10` means only that many runs got there; the median is over those runs.
