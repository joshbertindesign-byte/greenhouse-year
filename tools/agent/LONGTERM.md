# Greenhouse Year: long-term study (50 runs, until failure or year 40)

Five simulated players, 10 runs each, played until the nursery closed or 40 in-game years. Build: greenhouses capped at 8, specialty houses (vegetable house from level 8 with an olericulturist, propagation house from level 13 with a propagator), harder economy (wages +3%/yr, rising house upkeep, income tax after the 3-year tax break, crowded markets), harder late goals with multi-year streaks, rivals that keep improving, grower XP −15%. The agent converts a vegetable house at 3+ houses and a propagation house at 5+, and hires their specialists. Times are median **real play time at 1x speed** (running periods plus modeled planning, homestead and year-end time). `· 7/10` means only that many runs got there; the median is over those runs.
